    Ride every flight path

    Every flight connection your faction can use, flown once in each direction. This is the problem Atlas was written for: cover every edge of a graph in one walk. Three ways to do it, on the same map:

    The comparison appears here.

    Atlas is Savaş Takan's algorithm, ported to Lua. The weighted Atlas scores chains by new edges, then by time, and can jump with teleports. The Rural Postman joins the flight network's pieces, balances it with a min-cost flow and walks an Euler path. The lower bound is every flight flown once.

    Coursework for Savaş Takan by Alparslan Tuğra Şahin. Fan-made and unofficial; not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. Every number on this page comes from the addon's own Lua code, running in your browser through the wasmoon Lua VM.

    Map positions are hand-placed approximations, and boat and skyship times are estimates. Forever's additions follow the community guides listed in the README.
