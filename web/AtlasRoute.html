    The road ahead

    Tick some quests and plan the journey. The route will be inked onto the map. You can also click any place on the map to travel there.

    Ride every flight path

    Every flight connection your faction can use, flown once in each direction. This is the problem Atlas was written for: cover every edge of a graph in one walk. Two runs of Atlas on the same map:

    The comparison appears here.

    Atlas is Savaş Takan's algorithm, ported to Lua. The 2020 run is his code as written: it covers every edge of the travel graph and counts each one as one step. Atlas with travel times covers the flight links and weighs every edge by its time, as the planner does for quests. The lower bound is every flight flown once.
